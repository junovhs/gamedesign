The Gameplay Engine of Grindstone (In "Therefore / But" Structure)
The board fills with colorful Creeps that you can connect in single-color paths to clear them...
BUT your character (Jorj) doesn't just clear tiles remotely—he physically slashes along the drawn path and lands on the terminal tile.
THEREFORE, every move is not just a match; it is a physical relocation of your avatar across a spatial grid.
BUT single-color clusters are naturally small and fragmented, capping your maximum chain length and limiting your mobility.
THEREFORE, if you chain a long enough path (10+ Creeps), the game spawns a Grindstone gem onto the grid.
THEREFORE, you can route your path through a Grindstone to dynamically switch your active color mid-turn, linking disjointed color groups into massive, board-clearing mega-combos.
BUT at the end of every turn, un-cleared Creeps randomly turn Aggro and threaten all 8 tiles adjacent to them.
THEREFORE, if your path ends on a tile next to an Aggro Creep, you take damage—and you only have 3 hearts for the entire level.
THEREFORE, every single turn forces a high-stakes decision: Do you extend your combo to grab more resources, OR do you cut your combo short to land on a safe, unthreatened tile?
BUT mini-boss enemies ("Jerks") spawn with high HP (e.g., 10 HP) and cannot even be attacked unless your active chain momentum is already higher than their HP threshold.
THEREFORE, you can't just play passively or stay safe—you are forced to build massive, multi-color Grindstone chains across hostile board states just to accumulate enough raw momentum to break through high-HP blockers.
BUT spending turns building up complex multi-color setups leaves you vulnerable as more and more enemies turn Aggro around you with each passing turn.
THEREFORE, you must equip a specialized loadout of craftable gear—such as Shields to absorb attacks, Bows to snipe distant threat vectors, or Jump Potions to instantly reposition across the grid—to break the rules of the board when the systemic feedback loop corners you.
