Here is the gameplay of Critter Crunch rewritten entirely through the "Therefore / But" rule:
Swarms of wild critters invade Krunchatoa Island, THEREFORE Biggs must hunt them using his long, sticky tongue to harvest their internal jewels and feed his family.
Biggs pulls a small critter down from the hanging vines, THEREFORE he can spit it directly into a medium critter’s mouth to feed it.
BUT a single small critter isn't enough to satisfy a medium critter—it only turns it "Full."
THEREFORE, Biggs must grab another small critter and feed it to that same medium critter.
BUT even this second feeding doesn't clear the grid—it just turns the medium critter into a viable meal for a large critter.
THEREFORE, Biggs must pick up that Full medium critter and spit it into the mouth of a large critter.
THEREFORE, the large critter overeats and violently explodes!
BUT simply popping single critters one at a time won't fill Biggs' Hunger Bar fast enough before the creeping vines drop down to crush him.
THEREFORE, Biggs must group critters of the same color together so that a single explosion triggers a cascading chain reaction.
THEREFORE, executing a complete vertical "Food Chain" drops a glowing Bonus Coin that temporarily multiplies his point payouts.
BUT pulling off massive 8+ critter chain reactions causes Biggs' impatient son, "Smalls," to run onto the battlefield demanding food.
THEREFORE, Biggs must temporarily abandon line-clearing to walk over and barf a stream of rainbow nutrients directly into Smalls' mouth to claim a massive score bonus.
BUT in later levels, medium critters vanish and hazardous Bomber Critters begin spawning on the vines instead.
THEREFORE, Biggs must adapt his strategy by feeding any two random critters into a Bomber to set off a screen-clearing explosion.
BUT if Biggs pops the critters holding a bomb with nothing underneath it, the bomb drops to the ground and detonates directly on him.
THEREFORE, Biggs must constantly balance his spatial awareness, hazard management, and jewel collecting to fill his Hunger Bar before time runs out.
BUT if the player enters Puzzle Mode, the vines freeze and stop lowering completely.
THEREFORE, the game strips away real-time panic and transforms into a pure tactical brain-teaser, requiring Biggs to clear the entire grid within a strict move limit without making a single mistake.
